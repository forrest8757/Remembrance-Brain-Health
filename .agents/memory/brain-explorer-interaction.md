---
name: Brain explorer interaction direction
description: User-confirmed layout and transition preference for Remembrance’s wellness explorer.
---

Keep the five separate wellness-domain controls around the centered brain in a loose pentagon arrangement. When someone chooses a domain, hide the overview heading and surrounding controls, keep the same whole-brain illustration (not a region-specific image), move it upward and make it smaller, and bring the selected domain’s information up within the same explorer box rather than scrolling the visitor down to it.

**Why:** The user explicitly said they loved the pentagon concept, wanted a gradual in-place domain transition rather than a jump down the page, and later clarified that selection should not enter a specific brain section.

**How to apply:** Preserve this spatial overview-to-detail interaction when iterating on or integrating the Remembrance explorer. The initial approval was for a visual mockup, not an instruction to change production automatically.
