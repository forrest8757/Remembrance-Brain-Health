# Remembrance — Landing Page Spec (Waitlist)

**Purpose:** Convert a worried visitor into a waitlist signup (email only).
**Audience:** A person worried about *their own* brain health — usually someone who watched a parent or loved one go through cognitive decline. Emotion to speak to: fear born from experience + a desire for control. Tone: warm, reassuring, empowering. Never alarming or clinical.
**Positioning guardrail:** Wellness & monitoring only. No "detect," "diagnose," "screen," or "dementia test" language. Speak to *brain health*, *staying ahead of it*, *understanding how your brain is doing*.
**Brand:** Navy `#1E3A5F`, Cyan `#1BCEDF`, Cream `#F5F1EA`; existing gradient wordmark. Clean, spacious, senior-friendly (large type, high contrast, generous spacing).
**CTA everywhere:** Join the waitlist — **email capture only, no price shown.**

## Section 3 — How it works (the 3-step process)

**Headline:** Three simple steps to peace of mind.

**Step 1 — Take your baseline**
One easy assessment across five key areas of brain health gives you your starting point: your **Remembrance Score**.

**Step 2 — Check in weekly**
A few minutes a week. Each week focuses on one area of your brain, through quick, interactive activities and a short voice check.

**Step 3 — Get your plan & track progress**
Personalized guidance built around you — and a full re-score every five weeks so you can actually *see* your progress.

*(Visual: three clean cards or a horizontal stepper, each with a simple cyan icon. Numbered 1–2–3.)*
