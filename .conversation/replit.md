## Build intent and quality
Build a working mobile-first web app and waitlist page, not just a visual mockup. Prioritize exceptional interaction craft for adults often 45–70 who may be anxious or unfamiliar with technology. Navy #1E3A5F, Cyan #1BCEDF, Cream #F5F1EA; large readable type, generous space, high contrast, warm and restrained visual design.

Every assessment must have an uncounted guided warm-up, plain one-sentence instructions with optional audio, forgiving large touch targets, gentle feedback, pause/resume, clear progress, and calm pacing. Provide audio controls and reduced-motion support. Drawing must genuinely work with smooth strokes and undo; voice UI must gracefully handle unsupported or denied microphone access. Do not imply speech recognition occurred if scripted.

Implement baseline across five domains, composite /100, domain detail /20, five weekly domain tests and occasional in-app voice variant, shared ~30-second skippable processing, score reveal, dashboard, six-category care plans with accept/decline/swap-once, daily check-in, cycle comparison, and demo controls (reset, screen jumps, sample history, speed, score overrides).

## Honesty and safety
This release is the demo/MVP specified in the documents. Scores, voice analysis, and plans are simulated; clearly but discreetly label the demo and simulated results. Never present a simulated score as validated cognitive-health measurement or pretend actual biomarker analysis occurs.

Wellness-only copy throughout. No disease detection, diagnosis, screening, treatment, or guaranteed-prevention claims. Do not assert habits caused score changes. Include wellness disclaimers on assessments and plans. No unverified partnership badges, licensed assets, statistics, or compliance claims. Original non-branded task adaptations only.

Production medical/scoring validation, real biomarker analysis, real AI care engine, telephony, payments, and clinical features are out of scope. Do not confuse production-quality demo engineering with readiness to process real sensitive health data. Avoid collecting real health data in an unsecured demo.

Waitlist: email-only, no prices, genuine persisted signup with validation and success/error states. No sending marketing email or public deployment without authorization.
