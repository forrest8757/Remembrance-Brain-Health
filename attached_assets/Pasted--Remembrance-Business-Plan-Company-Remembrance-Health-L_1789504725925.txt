## 1. Executive summary

Remembrance is a direct-to-consumer brain-health platform that helps people track, understand, and support their cognitive health over time. Users take short weekly assessments across five brain domains, receive a single easy-to-read **Remembrance Score (/100)**, and follow personalized, AI-generated guidance across diet, exercise, sleep, social engagement, health checkups, and cognitive training. The product measures change over time so users can see whether the steps they take are helping.

Remembrance is positioned as a **wellness and monitoring** product — not a diagnostic or medical device. Its core user is an adult who watched a parent or loved one decline and wants agency over their own brain health.

This consumer product is one line within Remembrance Health LLC, which also operates a B2B beta and the Boost app (Dementia Society of America channel). The company has gone from $0 to ~$48K ARR in its first 90 days *(validate)* and holds an active partnership with the Dementia Society of America and a voice-biomarker partnership with Canary Speech.

## 2. The problem

- A large and growing population fears cognitive decline, especially those with a family history — a genuine, personal risk factor they can't un-know.
- Clinical cognitive assessment is episodic, intimidating, and disease-framed. People avoid it until something is clearly wrong.
- There's no accessible, non-alarming, longitudinal way for a worried individual to keep an eye on their brain health and act on it early.
- Lifestyle factors (diet, exercise, sleep, social engagement, cognitive activity) are widely linked to brain health, but people lack a personalized, trackable system that ties action to measurable change.

## 3. The solution

A consumer app that turns brain-health monitoring into a light weekly habit:

1. **Baseline** across all five domains → first Remembrance Score.
2. **Weekly domain check-ins** (interactive tasks + AI voice assessment), one domain at a time.
3. **Personalized guidance + progress tracking**, with a full re-score every five weeks to show change.

The value compounds: the longer someone uses it, the more longitudinal cognitive data and personalization the product accrues — the foundation of the company's long-term moat.

## 5. Product

See the PRD for full detail. Summary:
- Five domains (Complex Attention, Executive Function, Learning & Memory, Language, Perceptual-Motor), each /20 → composite /100.
- Live score; 5-week testing cycle with cycle-over-cycle progress.

- LLM care engine with rules guardrails across six lifestyle categories.
- Daily check-in + goals dashboard for engagement.

## 6. Business model

- **Model:** B2C subscription, **freemium → paid at $19.99/mo** *(validate against willingness-to-pay tests)*.
- **Free tier:** enough to hook — baseline, score, limited guidance.
- **Paid tier:** full weekly testing, full care engine, progress reports, ongoing tracking.
- **Acquisition:** worry-driven landing page + waitlist (email-only capture at this stage, price withheld), direct outreach, DSA-adjacent channels, and content aimed at the family-history audience.
- **Unit economics** *(build out):* target CAC by channel, expected retention curve, LTV at $19.99/mo, payback period. These need real numbers as the waitlist and early cohorts generate data.

## 7. Go-to-market

- **Phase 0 — Waitlist & demo:** ship the clickable demo, record a demo video, drive email signups, show it widely to gather signal and warm an early cohort.
- **Phase 1 — Early access:** convert waitlist to a paid or free-trial cohort; validate weekly engagement and the 5-week cycle; tune the care engine.
- **Phase 2 — Scale acquisition:** double down on the channels with the best CAC/retention; expand content and referral loops.

The consumer product also strengthens the company's broader strategy: longitudinal cognitive data as the moat, with future optionality toward clinical validation, proprietary models, and an eventual regulated pathway.

## 8. Competition

Mapped landscape includes Linus Health, Isaac Health, Canary Speech, Winterlight Labs, BrainCheck, Neurotrack, and Altoida. Most are clinical, B2B, or diagnostic-leaning. **Remembrance's differentiation:** a consumer-first, wellness-framed, longitudinal *habit* — not a one-off clinical screen — that pairs measurement with personalized action and tracks whether that action works. Comparable consumer-health analogy: Function Health (membership, data-forward, consumer-owned health tracking).

## 9. Regulatory & risk

- **Positioning discipline:** wellness and monitoring only. No detection/diagnosis/screening claims. This keeps the consumer product clear of the FDA medical-device pathway for now and reduces liability. Disclaimers throughout.
- **Clinical-instrument IP:** validated instruments (MoCA/SLUMS/Mini-Cog) carry licensing/certification requirements. Use open or original public-domain-based adaptations; legal review before production.
- **Data/privacy:** HIPAA-aligned handling; clear consent; sensitive health data protected.
- **Care-advice safety:** rules-based guardrails constrain LLM output; no medical advice, no dosing, escalation language toward professionals where appropriate.
