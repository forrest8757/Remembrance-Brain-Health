# Remembrance — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** 1.0 (Demo / MVP)
**Owner:** Drew — Founder & CEO, Remembrance Health LLC
**Status:** Draft for build
**Last updated:** September 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Remembrance is a direct-to-consumer brain-health platform. It helps people **track, understand, and support** their cognitive health over time through short weekly assessments, a single easy-to-read score, and personalized, AI-generated guidance.

This PRD covers the **consumer product**. It is a separate product line from the B2B beta and from Boost (the DSA-channel app).

**Positioning:** Wellness and monitoring. Remembrance is **not** a medical device and does **not** detect, diagnose, or screen for Alzheimer's, dementia, or any disease. All copy and features stay in the wellness lane.

---

## 2. Problem & audience

### Primary user
An adult (often 45–70) who is **worried about their own brain health** — typically because they watched a parent or loved one go through cognitive decline. They feel a mix of fear born from experience and a desire for control. They want to get ahead of it, not be blindsided.

### What they want
- Reassurance and a sense of agency ("I'm doing something about this")
- A simple, non-clinical way to keep an eye on how their brain is doing
- Clear, doable steps that might actually help
- To see progress over time

### What they don't want
- To be told they might have dementia
- A cold, clinical, alarming experience
- A 40-minute test or a giant intake form

---

## 3. Goals & non-goals

### Product goals
1. Give the user one clear number (the **Remembrance Score, /100**) that reflects their brain health and moves over time.
2. Make weekly engagement light, interesting, and habit-forming.
3. Deliver personalized guidance the user actually follows.
4. Show visible progress so the user feels the product is working.

### v1 (Demo) goal
Ship a **polished clickable demo** that looks and feels like the real product — enough to record a demo video and put in front of as many people as possible. Scoring and plans may be simulated under the hood; the experience must feel real.

### Non-goals for v1
- Live automated phone calls (voice test is in-app only for the demo)
- Real biomarker analysis / real scoring engine
- Real LLM plan generation (scripted/templated for demo is fine)
- Payments / subscription billing
- Clinician dashboards, caregiver accounts, B2B features

## 4. Core concepts

### 4.1 The five brain domains
Each domain is scored **/20**. The five composite to the /100 score.

| Domain | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Complex Attention | Sustained focus, processing speed, working attention |
| Executive Function | Planning, sequencing, mental flexibility, problem-solving |
| Learning & Memory | Encoding and recalling new information |
| Language | Naming, comprehension, verbal fluency |
| Perceptual-Motor | Visuospatial processing, coordination, construction |

*(Social Cognition — the sixth DSM-5 domain — is intentionally excluded from v1.)*

### 4.2 The Remembrance Score
- **Composite /100** = sum of the five domain scores (/20 each).
- The composite is the **headline number** on the dashboard.
- Per-domain **/20 scores + analysis** live **one level deeper** — visible only when the user clicks into a specific domain. They are not shown on the main dashboard.
- The score is **live**: completing any weekly domain test updates that domain's /20 and recalculates the composite immediately.

### 4.3 The testing cycle (5-week loop)
- **Week 0 — Baseline:** an all-domain assessment that touches all five domains and sets the first Remembrance Score.
- **Weeks 1–5 — Weekly domain tests:** one domain deep-tested per week, rotating through all five.
- **End of cycle:** once all five domains have been re-tested, the user gets a **cycle-over-cycle comparison** — "Since your last completed round of domain testing, here's how you've changed."
- Two views coexist: the **always-current score**, and the **full-cycle progress report** every 5 weeks.

### 4.4 Test modalities
- **Voice tests:** automated AI voice assessment. *Production:* Twilio + ElevenLabs. *Demo:* in-app voice prompt (mic UI, no telephony).
- **Interactive tests:** domain-targeted digital tasks taken in the web app (draw, match, tap, build, recall).
- Not every weekly test is voice — modality varies by domain and week.

> **Clinical-instrument licensing flag:** Interactive tests are modeled on validated instruments (MoCA, Mini-Cog, SLUMS). MoCA requires training/certification and SLUMS/Mini-Cog carry their own commercial-use terms. For production, use validated-but-open items or original adaptations of public-domain paradigms (same approach used to avoid Posit Science IP in Boost). **Legal review required before production launch.** Demo may use original, non-branded adaptations.

### 4.5 The AI care engine
- **v1 production:** LLM-generated personalized plans, with **rules-based guardrails** on top (safety limits, clinical no-go phrasing, dosage/claim constraints).
- **Six advice categories:** Diet · Aerobic/Exercise · Health Checkups · Sleep · Social Engagement · Cognitive Training.
- **Inputs to the engine:** domain scores + trends · intake (age, sex, education) · health context (existing conditions, medications, family history) · lifestyle (self-reported diet/exercise/sleep) · goal accept-decline history.
- **Onboarding is progressive:** collect only what's needed to generate the first plan up front; drip the rest across the first week to protect completion rates.
- *Demo:* plans are templated/scripted but presented as if freshly generated.

### 4.6 Goals & plans loop
- The engine issues **goals** and **plans** the user can **accept or decline**.
- **Decline behavior:** offer **one swap** (an alternative). If declined again, **log it and move on**.
- Accept/decline history feeds back into the engine so recommendations adapt to what the user will actually do.

### 4.7 Daily engagement loop
- **Daily check-in:** a quick, low-friction log (mood / sleep / how they're feeling).
- **Goals progress dashboard:** the user watches their goal progress move day to day.
- No streaks or forced gamification in v1.

## 5. User journey

1. **Land** → hero, worry-driven hook, 3-step explainer → join waitlist (email only) *(marketing site; see Landing Page Spec)*.
2. **Onboard** → account, essential intake, first plan generated.
3. **Baseline test** → all-domain assessment → first Remembrance Score reveal.
4. **Home dashboard** → composite score, today's check-in, today's goals, next test.
5. **Weekly domain test** → one domain, interactive or voice → ~30s processing sequence → score update + domain analysis.
6. **Care plan** → personalized guidance across the six categories → accept/decline goals.
7. **Daily return** → check-in + goal tracking.
8. **End of 5-week cycle** → full-cycle progress comparison report.

---

## 6. Feature requirements

### 6.1 Onboarding & intake
- Account creation (demo: can be a name + email, no real auth needed).
- Progressive intake: essentials first (age, sex, education, top concern), rest dripped over week 1.
- Health-context capture (conditions, meds, family history) framed gently and optional-friendly.

### 6.2 Assessment engine (demo-simulated)
- Baseline (all-domain) flow.
- Five weekly domain flows (see Product/Demo Build Spec for each test).
- In-app voice-prompt test with mic capture UI.
- **~30-second rich processing sequence** after each test: waveform/metrics animation + the relevant domain lighting up on the five-sector brain visualization → dramatic reveal. **Must be skippable** (skip tap or demo-mode timing control) so live demos aren't stuck watching it every time.

### 6.3 Scoring & dashboard
- Composite /100 as headline, with the five-sector brain viz.
- Click into a domain → /20 + written analysis + trend.
- Live recalculation on each completed test.
- Cycle-over-cycle comparison report at end of cycle.

### 6.4 Care engine & plans
- Plan presentation across six categories.
- Goal cards with accept / decline → swap-once logic.
- Plan feels freshly generated per user (demo: templated).

### 6.5 Daily loop
- Daily check-in widget.
- Goals progress dashboard.

## 7. Data model (core entities)

- **User** — profile, intake, health context, lifestyle.
- **DomainScore** — user, domain, score /20, timestamp, test reference.
- **RemembranceScore** — user, composite /100, timestamp (derived/snapshotted).
- **TestSession** — user, domain(s), modality (voice/interactive), raw result, computed score, timestamp.
- **Cycle** — user, cycle number, start/end, per-domain scores, comparison summary.
- **Plan** — user, category, content, generated_at, status.
- **Goal** — user, plan, description, status (accepted/declined/swapped), history.
- **CheckIn** — user, date, mood/sleep/notes.

---

## 8. Non-functional requirements

- **Accessibility:** large touch targets, high contrast, legible type, senior-friendly. WCAG-minded (the audience skews older).
- **Tone:** warm, reassuring, empowering — never alarming or clinical.
- **Brand:** Navy `#1E3A5F`, Cyan `#1BCEDF`, Cream `#F5F1EA`; existing gradient wordmark.
- **Performance:** processing animations feel intentional, not laggy.
- **Compliance (production):** HIPAA-compliant data handling; wellness disclaimers present throughout; "not a medical device / not a substitute for professional care" language on assessments and plans.

---

## 9. Success metrics

**Demo phase:** demo completes cleanly on video; positive reactions from viewers; waitlist signups from the marketing page.

**Post-launch (future):** activation (baseline completed), weekly test completion rate, daily check-in rate, goal acceptance rate, 5-week cycle completion, month-2 retention, free→paid conversion.

---

## 10. Out of scope for v1 (parking lot)

Live Twilio/ElevenLabs calls · real biomarker analysis · real LLM engine + guardrail system · real scoring algorithm & normative data · Social Cognition domain · payments/subscription · caregiver/family accounts · clinician view · licensed clinical instruments (pending legal) · push/SMS notifications · streaks/gamification.
